📌 How U.S. Visa Sponsorship Works – H1B, H2B, EB3 Explained

The U.S. has multiple employer‑sponsored pathways. Here is the real process for the most common visas:

  1. Employer obtains Labor Certification (PERM for green card or LCA for H1B/H2B) – The employer must prove no qualified U.S. worker is available (for some visas).
  2. Job offer & visa petition (Form I‑129) – The employer files a petition with USCIS on your behalf.
  3. Visa application & interview (U.S. embassy/consulate) – After approval, you apply for the visa stamp in your home country.
  4. Arrival & compliance – You enter the U.S. and maintain your visa status (e.g., H1B allows dual intent).
  5. Path to green card (EB2/EB3) – Some visas lead to permanent residency.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – U.S. Work Visas

Can foreigners get jobs in America with visa sponsorship?
Yes, thousands of U.S. employers sponsor H1B, H2B, and EB3 visas each year. IFMOSA lists only verified sponsorship opportunities.
What is the easiest U.S. work visa to get?
H2B and H2A (seasonal) have lower entry barriers but are time‑limited. For professionals, H1B is the most common route.
Do I need a job offer before applying for a U.S. work visa?
Yes, almost all employment‑based visas require a confirmed job offer from a U.S. employer. IFMOSA connects you directly to such employers.
How long does U.S. work visa processing take?
H1B: 3–6 months (premium processing 15 days). H2B/H2A: 2–4 months. EB3 green card: 1–3 years (priority date dependent).
Is IFMOSA a recruiter or job board?
IFMOSA is a verified job board. We never charge placement fees. You apply directly to employers. Go membership provides premium tools, but the application remains between you and the sponsor.
What are common U.S. job scams?
Requests for upfront visa fees, “guaranteed H1B” promises without employer petition, and fake USCIS documents. Always verify employer on the DOL website.

⚠️ Common U.S. Job Scams – How to Spot Fake Sponsorship

  • Upfront payment for “visa processing”: Legitimate U.S. employers pay filing fees, not the employee.
  • “Guaranteed H1B” without lottery: H1B is a lottery system – no one can guarantee selection.
  • Fake USCIS receipt numbers: Always check cases on the official USCIS website.
