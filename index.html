Frequently Asked Questions

What is a visa‑sponsored job?
A visa‑sponsored job is one where the employer is licensed by the government to sponsor your work permit or visa, handling the legal paperwork so you can live and work abroad.
How do I find an employer that sponsors visas?
Use IFMOSA to access our database of verified sponsors. We list direct contact details – no middlemen, no recruitment agencies.
Which country is easiest to get a work visa?
Canada (LMIA‑exempt jobs under Global Talent Stream), Germany (EU Blue Card with reduced salary for IT), and Australia (SID 482 with 1 year experience) are currently the most accessible.
Do I need a job offer before applying for a work visa?
Yes – for almost all work visas (LMIA, H‑1B, Skilled Worker, TSS 482) a confirmed job offer from a licensed sponsor is mandatory. IFMOSA connects you directly to those employers.
