📌 How Kuwait Work Visa Sponsorship Works

Kuwait's Public Authority for Manpower (PAM) manages work permits. Steps:

  1. Employer obtains work permit from PAM.
  2. Job offer and contract signed (Arabic required; English translation recommended).
  3. Employer sponsors entry visa (valid 60 days).
  4. After arrival: medical test (blood, chest X‑ray), fingerprinting, and Civil ID issuance.

Official portal: Kuwait PAM.

🏗️ High‑Demand Jobs in Kuwait (2026)

  • Oil & Gas – rig workers, technicians, engineers.
  • Construction – labourers, carpenters, electricians, drivers.
  • Healthcare – nurses, lab technicians, medical assistants.
  • Hospitality – hotel staff, chefs, waiters.
  • Domestic work – nannies, housemaids (special sponsorship).

❓ Kuwait Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree to work in Kuwait?
Not for construction, cleaning, or hospitality. Healthcare and oil & gas require qualifications.
How long does the Kuwait work visa take?
3‑8 weeks for entry visa; in‑country processing 1‑3 weeks (medical test is strict).
Can I bring my family?
Yes, if your salary exceeds KWD 350 (approx).
