📌 EU Blue Card – Europe's Work Permit for Professionals

The EU Blue Card is a residence permit for highly qualified non-EU citizens. 2026 thresholds:

  • Germany: €50,700 (€45,934 for shortage occupations)
  • Netherlands: €5,942/month (€4,754 for graduates)
  • Luxembourg: €65,652 (effective March 3, 2026)
  • Cyprus: €43,632 (from July 2025)
  • Czech Republic: CZK 77,245/month

Benefits: family reunification, job mobility within EU, faster permanent residence (33 months, or 21 months with B1 language).

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 How to Bring Your Family – Family Reunification Process

Most EU countries allow family reunification after you obtain a work permit or EU Blue Card. General steps:

  1. Secure your own residence permit (work visa or EU Blue Card).
  2. Apply for family reunification at the local immigration office (usually after 3‑12 months of legal residence).
  3. Provide proof of sufficient income (minimum salary threshold varies by country).
  4. Provide adequate housing (size and standards).
  5. Family members (spouse, children under 18) receive residence permit with work rights.

👉 Each country guide includes specific requirements and processing times for family reunification.

❓ Europe Work Visa FAQ

Which country has the fastest visa processing?
Netherlands (2‑4 weeks), Germany (8‑12 weeks), Poland (1‑3 months).
Can I bring my family immediately?
Some countries require 3‑12 months of residence before family reunification. Check country‑specific guides.
What is the cheapest European country to relocate to?
Poland, Portugal, and Spain have the lowest living costs.

⚠️ Common Europe Job Scams

  • Upfront payment for “visa processing” – legitimate employers do not ask for money.
  • Fake employment contracts – verify company registration in local business registry.
  • WhatsApp recruiters asking for passport scans without a contract.
