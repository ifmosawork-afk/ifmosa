📌 How Bahrain Work Visa Sponsorship Works

Bahrain's LMRA (Labour Market Regulatory Authority) manages work permits. Steps:

  1. Employer obtains work permit quota from LMRA.
  2. You sign a labour contract (must be registered in the online system).
  3. Employer sponsors entry visa (valid 30 days).
  4. After arrival: medical test, fingerprinting, and CPR (Central Population Registry) card issuance.

Official portal: LMRA Bahrain.

❓ Bahrain Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree to work in Bahrain?
Not for many roles. Finance and healthcare may require qualifications.
How long does the Bahrain work visa take?
2‑6 weeks for entry visa; in‑country processing about 1 week.
Can I bring my family?
Yes, if your salary exceeds BHD 400 (approx).
