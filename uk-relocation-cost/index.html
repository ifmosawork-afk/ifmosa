✈️ Pre‑Departure & Travel Costs (One‑Time)

  • Flight to the UK: £500–£1,500 (depending on origin, season, airline).
  • Excess baggage / shipping belongings: £200–£800.
  • Travel insurance (first month): £30–£80 – recommended until you register with a GP.
  • Currency exchange / bank transfer fees: £20–£100.
  • UK SIM card or eSIM (first month): £10–£30.

✈️ Employer Relocation Packages – What to Expect

  • Visa fees: Some employers cover visa and IHS costs (rare, but possible for senior roles).
  • Flight tickets: One‑way economy for you and family (common in tech and healthcare).
  • Temporary accommodation: 1‑2 months in a serviced apartment (value £2,000‑£5,000).
  • Relocation allowance: £2,000‑£5,000 cash for initial expenses (common for in‑demand professions).
  • Settlement support: Assistance with council tax registration, bank account setup, and National Insurance number.

⏱️ Processing Time & Family Reunification

Processing time: Standard visa: 8 weeks (outside UK), 3‑4 weeks (priority), 1 day (super priority).

Family reunification: Dependants (spouse, children under 18) can apply at the same time or later. You must prove sufficient income and adequate accommodation. Spouses receive work rights without restrictions. Processing: 2‑3 months.

🎯 Who This Guide Is For

This guide is for skilled professionals – IT specialists, engineers, healthcare workers, finance professionals – planning to relocate to the UK on a Skilled Worker visa. Use it to budget your move, compare London vs regional costs, and negotiate relocation packages.

❓ UK Relocation FAQ

How much money should I have before moving to the UK?
For a single person to a regional city: £10,000–£12,000 after visa fees. London: £18,000–£22,000.
Can my employer help with relocation costs?
Some offer relocation allowances (£2,000–£5,000) for in‑demand professions.
What is the cheapest city to live in the UK?
Cardiff, Newcastle, Sheffield, Belfast – rent 40‑50% lower than London.
Is the Immigration Health Surcharge refundable?
Yes, if your visa is refused or you leave early, you can claim a refund for unused full 6‑month periods.
