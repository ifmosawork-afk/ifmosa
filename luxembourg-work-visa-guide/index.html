📌 Luxembourg Work Visas – Overview

  • EU Blue Card: For highly qualified workers. Minimum annual salary €65,652 (effective 3 March 2026). Shorter path to permanent residence.
  • Standard Work Permit (Salaried Worker): For non‑EU workers with a job offer. Employer must prove the role cannot be filled locally.
  • ICT Permit: For intra‑company transfers (managers, specialists, trainees).
  • Processing time: 2‑3 months for work permit; 6‑10 weeks for EU Blue Card.

🧾 Tax & Take‑Home Pay – Luxembourg Tax System

Luxembourg has progressive income tax (8‑42%). Social security contributions ~14% (shared). Example: Gross €70,000 → net ≈ €48,000 (≈ €4,000/month). No special expat tax scheme, but salaries are high enough to offset taxes.

💰 Can Professionals Save Money in Luxembourg?

Example: IT professional with €75,000 gross annual salary in Luxembourg City.

  • Net monthly income: ≈ €4,200
  • Monthly expenses: Rent €1,800, groceries €400, transport €50, utilities €200, internet €50 = €2,500
  • Monthly savings: ≈ €1,700 (≈ €20,400/year). Living on the French or German border (commuting) increases savings significantly.

🌟 Why Professionals Choose Luxembourg

  • Highest minimum wage in Europe (€2,638/month gross)
  • Excellent social security (healthcare, pension, unemployment)
  • Multilingual environment (French, German, English widely spoken)
  • Central location – easy travel to Belgium, France, Germany
  • Pathway to permanent residence after 5 years
  • Citizenship after 5 years (with Luxembourgish language test)

🟢 Long‑Term Career & Permanent Residence

  • Permanent residence after 5 years of continuous legal residence.
  • Citizenship after 5 years (with language exam in Luxembourgish).
  • Salary growth: 10‑15% annual increases in finance/IT.
  • European mobility after PR (EU long‑term residence).

🏙️ Most Popular Cities for Expats

  • Luxembourg City: Largest job market, most expats, highest rent.
  • Esch‑sur‑Alzette: Second largest, lower rent, growing tech hub.
  • Kirchberg (district): EU institutions, finance, modern housing.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 How to Bring Your Family

  • Eligibility: You have a residence permit valid for at least 1 year.
  • Timing: Can apply immediately after you receive your permit.
  • Income requirement: Sufficient income (above social welfare).
  • Documents: marriage certificate (apostille), birth certificates.
  • Processing: 2‑3 months. Family members receive work rights.

❓ Luxembourg Visa FAQ

Do I need French or German?
Many finance/IT roles require only English. EU institutions often require French/English.
Is public transport really free?
Yes, since 2020, all public transport (buses, trains, trams) is free throughout Luxembourg.
How fast can I get permanent residence?
After 5 years of legal residence.
