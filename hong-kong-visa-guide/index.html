📌 Hong Kong Work Visas – Overview

  • Employment Visa (Professionals): Requires a job offer from a Hong Kong employer. Must have a bachelor’s degree and relevant work experience. No quota, no labour market test. Valid for 1‑2 years, renewable. Pathway to permanent residence after 7 years.
  • Working Holiday Visa: For young adults (18‑30) from select countries (Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, Japan, Korea, UK, USA, etc.). Valid for 12 months. Allows work (any employer) but no more than 3 months with the same employer. No sponsorship needed – apply independently.
  • Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS): For high‑income earners (HK$2.5M+ annual) or graduates of top 100 universities. Fast‑track.

Official source: Hong Kong Immigration Department.

📝 How to Apply – Employment Visa

  1. Secure a job offer from a Hong Kong employer.
  2. Employer submits visa application to Immigration Department (Form ID 990A + supporting docs).
  3. Processing time: 4‑6 weeks (can be expedited).
  4. If approved, you receive visa label. Enter Hong Kong within 3 months.
  5. Register for Hong Kong ID card within 30 days of arrival.

❓ Hong Kong Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree for Hong Kong work visa?
Yes, for Employment Visa – a bachelor’s degree is mandatory. Working Holiday visa does not require a degree.
Is English enough?
Yes, English is widely used in business. Mandarin is an advantage.
Can I bring my family?
Yes, Employment Visa holders can bring spouse and children (Dependent visa).
How long does it take to get PR?
After 7 years of continuous ordinary residence.
