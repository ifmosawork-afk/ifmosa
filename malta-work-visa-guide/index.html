Single Permit, Pre‑Departure Course & Key Employee Initiative (KEI)

📌 Malta Work Visas – Overview & 2026 Changes

  • Single Permit: Combined work and residence permit for third‑country nationals (TCNs). Valid 1‑2 years, renewable.
  • Pre‑Departure Course (NEW from 1 March 2026): Mandatory for all first‑time applicants. Fee €250. Certificate must be presented before visa application.
  • Key Employee Initiative (KEI): Fast‑track processing for roles with annual gross salary ≥ €45,000. Reduced waiting times (2‑3 weeks).
  • EU Blue Card: For highly qualified professionals. Minimum annual salary ≈ €36,000.
  • Processing time: Standard: 2‑4 months (after course completion); KEI: 2‑3 weeks.

🧾 Tax & Take‑Home Pay – Maltese Tax System

Malta has progressive income tax (15% first €15,000, then 25% up to €60,000, 35% above). Social security contributions ~10%. Example: Gross €45,000 → net ≈ €31,500 (≈ €2,625/month). No special expat tax scheme, but the cost of living is moderate.

💰 Can Professionals Save Money in Malta?

Example: iGaming professional with €50,000 gross annual salary in St. Julian's.

  • Net monthly income: ≈ €3,200
  • Monthly expenses: Rent €1,500, groceries €400, transport €60, utilities €150, internet €50 = €2,160
  • Monthly savings: ≈ €1,040 (≈ €12,500/year). Living in Birkirkara or lower‑cost areas increases savings.

⏱️ Processing Time Frame

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 How to Bring Your Family

  • Eligibility: You have a Single Permit valid for at least 1 year.
  • Timing: Usually after 1 year of legal residence (immediate for KEI or Blue Card holders).
  • Income requirement: Sufficient income (above minimum wage).
  • Documents: marriage certificate (apostille), birth certificates.
  • Processing: 2‑3 months. Family members receive work rights.

❓ Malta Visa FAQ

What is the Pre‑Departure Course?
A mandatory online course for first‑time applicants, costing €250. It covers Maltese culture, laws, and integration. Required from 1 March 2026.
Do I need English?
Yes, English is required for most jobs (iGaming, IT, hospitality). No formal test for the visa.
What is the Key Employee Initiative (KEI)?
Fast‑track processing for roles with annual salary ≥ €45,000. Reduces waiting time to 2‑3 weeks.
