📌 How Qatar Work Visa Sponsorship Works

Qatar operates a standard employer‑sponsored system. Steps:

  1. Employer obtains a work permit quota from the Ministry of Labour (ADLSA).
  2. You receive a job offer and sign a labour contract (in Arabic and English).
  3. Employer sponsors an entry visa (valid 60 days).
  4. After arrival, you undergo medical tests, biometrics, and obtain a Qatar ID (QID).
  5. Residence permit issued – you can now work legally.

Official portal: ADLSA Qatar.

❓ Qatar Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree to work in Qatar?
Not for most unskilled/semi‑skilled roles. Skilled jobs (nursing, engineering) require qualifications.
How long does the Qatar work visa take?
2‑8 weeks from job offer to entry visa; in‑country processing 1‑2 weeks.
Can I bring my family?
Yes, if your salary exceeds QAR 10,000 (approx). Many skilled roles qualify.
