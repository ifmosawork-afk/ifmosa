📌 How Saudi Work Visa Sponsorship Works (Qiwa)

Saudi Arabia uses the Qiwa platform for all work visa processes. Steps:

  1. Employer gets work permit quota from Ministry of Human Resources (MHRSD).
  2. Job offer and contract signed (must be in Arabic and English).
  3. Employer issues entry visa (valid 90 days).
  4. After arrival, medical test, fingerprinting, and Iqama (residence permit) issuance.

Official portal: Qiwa Saudi Arabia.

❓ Saudi Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree to work in Saudi?
Not for construction, cleaning, hospitality. Skilled roles require qualifications.
How long does the Saudi work visa take?
3‑8 weeks for entry visa; in‑country processing 1‑2 weeks.
Can I bring my family?
Yes, if your salary exceeds SAR 4,000 and you meet other criteria.
