Singapore Work Visa Guide 2026

S Pass, Employment Pass (EP) & Work Permit – Sponsorship & Benefits

Singapore offers three main work passes: Work Permit (semi‑skilled), S Pass (mid‑skilled), and Employment Pass (professionals). This guide covers eligibility, quotas, salaries, and benefits including housing, transport, and healthcare.

📌 Singapore Work Passes – Overview

  • Work Permit (WP): For semi‑skilled workers (construction, manufacturing, marine, domestic). Quota and levy apply. No minimum salary but employer‑specific. No PR pathway.
  • S Pass (SP): For mid‑skilled staff (technicians, supervisors). Minimum salary S$3,150 (2026). Quota up to 10% of company workforce. Levy applies. Can bring family if salary > S$6,000.
  • Employment Pass (EP): For professionals (managers, executives, specialists). Minimum salary S$5,000 (S$5,500 for financial services). Points‑based COMPASS framework. No quota but needs to pass points test. Family and PR pathways.
  • Personalised Employment Pass (PEP): For high‑earners (S$12,000+). Not tied to an employer.

Official source: MOM Singapore.

  • 🚌 Employer provides transport or transport allowance (common in construction and manufacturing).
  • 🩺 Mandatory medical coverage (basic outpatient and inpatient).
  • 💰 Central Provident Fund (CPF) contributions for Singapore PRs and citizens – not for foreign work pass holders.
  • ✅ Zero personal income tax on first S$20,000; progressive above that (still low compared to West).

📝 How to Apply – Step‑by‑Step

  1. Find an employer with an available quota (for WP and S Pass).
  2. Employer submits application via MOM’s online portal (WP Online / EP eService).
  3. If approved, you receive an In‑Principle Approval (IPA) letter.
  4. Enter Singapore (employment pass holder can enter with IPA).
  5. Complete medical exam and fingerprinting (within 2 weeks).
  6. Collect your work pass card (issued within 5 working days).

Processing time: 1–3 weeks for S Pass/EP, 1 week for Work Permit.

❓ Singapore Work Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree for S Pass?
No. A diploma or technical certificate is sufficient. EP requires a degree.
What is the S Pass quota?
Up to 10% of a company’s total workforce (2026). Higher for services sector.
Can I bring my family?
EP holders with salary ≥ S$6,000 can bring spouse and children (Dependant’s Pass). S Pass requires ≥ S$6,000 as well.
How long does the visa last?
Work Permit: up to 2 years, renewable. S Pass / EP: initially 1‑2 years, renewable.
