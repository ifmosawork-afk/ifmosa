📌 Asia Work Visa Overview – Key Pathways

🇸🇬 Singapore: Work Permit (semi‑skilled), S Pass (mid‑skilled, S$3,150+), Employment Pass (professionals, S$5,000+). Quotas and levy apply. No language test.
🇯🇵 Japan: Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) visa for 14 sectors. Requires skills test + Japanese N4 language exam. No degree needed. Pathway to PR after 5 years.
🇨🇳 China: Z visa (work visa) requires a job offer, bachelor’s degree + 2 years experience (or TEFL for teachers). Points‑based system (A/B/C categories).
🇻🇳 Vietnam: Work permit required for most foreign workers. Needs a university degree + 3 years experience (or relevant technical skills). Visa exemption for some nationalities.
🇭🇰 Hong Kong: Employment visa for professionals (degree + relevant experience). No quota. Also Working Holiday visa for young adults from select countries.
🇹🇭 Thailand: Non‑Immigrant B visa for employment. Requires job offer and work permit. Minimum salary based on nationality (e.g., ฿50,000 for most Westerners).
🇹🇷 Turkey: Work permit requires job offer and employer’s approval from Ministry of Labour. Foreigners limited to 20% per company (recently reduced).

👷 Unskilled & Semi‑Skilled Workers – Visa Sponsorship Options

  • Singapore: Work Permit for construction, domestic work, cleaning, manufacturing. No degree required.
  • Japan: SSW visa for nursing care, agriculture, food processing, construction. Skills test + N4 Japanese.
  • Vietnam: Work permit possible for skilled trades (e.g., welders, technicians) with experience.
  • Hong Kong: Foreign domestic helpers (special visa category).
  • Thailand & Turkey: Limited – most roles require a degree or specialised skills.

🏢 Employer Sponsorship – What You Should Know

  • In most Asian countries, the employer applies for your work permit or visa (e.g., MOM in Singapore, SAFEA in China).
  • You should never pay for visa processing fees – legitimate employers cover these costs.
  • Contracts must clearly state salary, accommodation, working hours, and termination conditions.
  • Always verify employer registration: ACRA for Singapore, GBizID for Japan, local business license for China.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Asia Work Visas

Which Asian country is easiest to get a work visa?
Singapore (S Pass) and Japan (SSW) are relatively accessible. For unskilled workers, Singapore Work Permit or Japan SSW are best.
Do I need to speak the local language?
Singapore – English is enough. Japan – basic Japanese (N4) required for SSW. China – English is fine for teaching, but Mandarin helps. Vietnam, Thailand, Turkey – local language not mandatory but beneficial.
How long does processing take?
Singapore: 1–3 weeks. Japan: 1–3 months (including exams). China: 2–3 months. Vietnam: 1–2 months. Hong Kong: 4–6 weeks. Thailand: 2–3 months. Turkey: 2–4 months.
Can I bring my family?
Singapore – yes for EP and S Pass (salary dependent). Japan – yes for SSW Type ii (higher skilled). China – yes for Z visa (Category A/B). Others depend on salary.
Is there a path to permanent residence?
Singapore – after 2‑6 years (EP). Japan – after 5 years (SSW Type ii). China – after 4 years (Category A). Hong Kong – after 7 years. Thailand – after 3 years on work permit.

⚠️ Common Asia Job Scams – How to Spot Fake Sponsorship

  • Upfront payment for “visa processing”: No legitimate employer asks for money before hiring.
  • “Guaranteed visa” promises: Only government immigration authorities decide.
  • Fake MOM letters (Singapore) or visa reservation numbers: Always verify through official portals.
  • WhatsApp recruiters asking for passport scans without a contract: Legitimate employers provide a job offer first.
