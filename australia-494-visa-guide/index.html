Australia Subclass 494 Visa Guide 2026

Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional – Pathway to Permanent Residence

The Subclass 494 visa (Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional) allows employers in regional Australia to sponsor skilled workers for up to 5 years. After living and working in a designated regional area for 3 years, you may apply for permanent residence (Subclass 191). This guide covers eligibility, occupation lists, salary thresholds, relocation costs, and family reunification.

📌 Subclass 494 Visa – Overview

  • Purpose: To address skill shortages in designated regional areas of Australia (all areas except Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane).
  • Validity: Up to 5 years.
  • Streams: Employer Sponsored stream (main) and Labour Agreement stream.
  • Pathway to PR: After 3 years of living and working in a regional area on a 494 visa, you can apply for the Subclass 191 (Permanent Residence – Skilled Regional) visa.
  • Processing time: 3‑6 months (priority processing for regional areas).

Official source: Australian Department of Home Affairs – Subclass 494.

✅ Eligibility Requirements

  • Occupation: Must be on the Regional Occupation List (ROL) or combined list for regional.
  • Job offer: A genuine full‑time position (at least 35 hours/week) in a designated regional area from an approved sponsor.
  • Skills assessment: Required for most occupations (unless exempt).
  • Work experience: At least 3 years of full‑time work experience in the nominated occupation (or equivalent part‑time).
  • Age: Under 45 years at the time of application (age exemptions may apply).
  • English: Competent English – IELTS 6.0 in each band (or equivalent).
  • Salary: Must meet the Temporary Skilled Migration Income Threshold (TSMIT) – currently A$73,150 (2026), or the market rate, whichever is higher.
  • Health and character: Standard health exams and police certificates.

🧾 Tax & Take‑Home Pay – Australian Tax System

Australia has progressive income tax (0% up to $18,200, 16% up to $45,000, 30% up to $120,000, 37% up to $180,000, 45% above). Medicare levy is 2%. Example: Gross $80,000 → net ≈ $64,000 (≈ $5,333/month). Regional areas may offer lower living costs, increasing savings potential.

✈️ Employer Relocation Packages for Regional Sponsorship

⏱️ Processing Time & Family Reunification

Processing time: 3‑6 months (regional priority processing).

Family reunification: You can include your spouse/partner and dependent children in the application. Family members receive full work and study rights in Australia. Additional costs: approx $2,000‑$3,000 per dependent.

🟢 Pathway to Permanent Residence – Subclass 191 (Skilled Regional)

  • After holding a Subclass 494 visa for 3 years and living/working in a designated regional area.
  • Meet income requirements (≥ TSMIT $73,150/year for at least 3 years).
  • No age restriction for 191 visa (unlike other PR visas).
  • After 1 year of PR, you can apply for Australian citizenship (subject to residency requirements).

❓ Subclass 494 FAQ

What is the difference between 494 and 482?
494 is specifically for regional employers and leads to PR after 3 years. 482 (medium‑term) leads to PR after 3 years but can be for metropolitan areas as well.
Can I change employers on a 494 visa?
Yes, but you must find a new employer in a regional area who is willing to sponsor you and apply for a new nomination.
Do I need a skills assessment?
Yes for most occupations. Some labour agreement roles may waive it.
Can I live in any regional area?
Yes, anywhere designated as “regional” (postcodes excluding Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane).
