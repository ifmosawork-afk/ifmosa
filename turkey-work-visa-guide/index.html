Turkey Work Visa Guide 2026

Work Permit – Employer Sponsorship & 20% Foreign Worker Quota

Turkey requires a work permit for foreign employees, and the employer must apply on your behalf. The government limits foreign workers to 20% of a company’s workforce (recently reduced). This guide covers eligibility, sponsorship, salary expectations, relocation costs, family reunification, and the step‑by‑step application process.

📌 Turkey Work Visas – Overview

  • Work Permit (Çalışma İzni): Required for all foreign employees. Valid for 1 year initially, then renewable up to 3‑year increments.
  • Employer obligation: Employer must prove no suitable Turkish citizen is available, and foreign employee quota is 20% of workforce (reduced from 50% in previous years).
  • Exemptions: Some professions (academic researchers, certain artists) may be exempt.
  • Processing time: 2‑4 months.

🧾 Tax & Take‑Home Pay – Turkish Tax System

Progressive income tax (15‑40%). Social security contributions ~14% (employee). Example: Gross ₺50,000/month → net ≈ ₺32,000/month.

💰 Can Professionals Save Money in Turkey?

Example: Software Engineer with ₺60,000/month gross in Istanbul.

  • Net monthly income: ≈ ₺36,000
  • Monthly expenses: Rent ₺15,000, groceries ₺6,000, transport ₺1,500, utilities ₺2,500, internet ₺500 = ₺25,500
  • Monthly savings: ≈ ₺10,500 (≈ €350 / ₺126,000 per year).

⏱️ Processing Time & Family Reunification

Work permit: 2‑4 months. Family reunification: spouses and children can apply for family residence permit after you obtain work permit. Processing: 2‑3 months.

❓ Turkey Visa FAQ

Do I need Turkish language?
No for most tech roles. Basic Turkish helps daily life.
What is the foreign worker quota?
Foreign employees cannot exceed 20% of company workforce (2026).
Can I apply for permanent residence?
After 8 years of continuous legal residence.
