📌 How Oman Work Visa Sponsorship Works

Oman's Ministry of Labour manages work permits. Steps:

  1. Employer obtains work permit quota.
  2. Job offer and contract signed (Arabic required, English translation advised).
  3. Employer sponsors entry visa (valid 30‑60 days).
  4. After arrival: medical test, fingerprinting, and resident card (Civil ID) issuance.

Official portal: Oman Ministry of Labour.

❓ Oman Visa FAQ

Do I need a degree to work in Oman?
Not for many roles. Healthcare and engineering require qualifications.
How long does the Oman work visa take?
3‑8 weeks for entry visa; in‑country processing 1‑2 weeks.
Can I bring my family?
Yes, if your salary exceeds OMR 600 (approx).
